Questions that come up often

  • Does my data go to a server? No. It stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. Nothing passes through me.
  • Can I use it on more than one device? Yes. With iCloud on, iPhone, iPad and Mac stay in sync on their own, through your Apple Account.
  • How do I take my data with me? In Settings → Export everything the app builds a spreadsheet with every entry.
  • How do I erase everything? In Settings → Delete everything and start over. It clears every synced device and cannot be undone.
  • I lost my device. With iCloud on, install Aprumo on the new one using the same Apple Account and your data comes back.
  • What does it cost? There is a monthly plan and a yearly one. The price for your region is shown by the App Store before you buy, and downloading the app costs nothing.
  • What if I cancel? The app still opens. You can read and export everything you have already recorded; what stops is recording new things. Details in the Terms of Use.
  • How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aprumo. Cancelling happens in your Apple Account, not with me.
